Strategies for a Seamless Rollout

What helps your programme run smoothly

  • Engage Early: Partner with us during the initial town planning phase, preferably before submitting development applications
  • Sync Contractors: Align our civil deployment teams with your head contractors right from the start
  • Parallel Approvals: Process electrical approvals concurrently with civil network design, rather than consecutively
  • Fast-Track Forms: Initiate Form 1 and 2 requests early—as soon as land titles fall on the critical path
  • Turnkey Handover: Rely on our team to fully test and commission the network so everything is active before residents arrive

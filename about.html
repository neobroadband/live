Who We Are

Neo Broadband is an independent Australian telecommunications company built for the modern digital era. We provide ultra-reliable, high-speed internet connections tailored specifically for households and evolving workplaces.

Whether you're streaming 4K entertainment at home, running a busy office, or managing a remote workforce, we ensure your connection remains seamless, secure, and lightning-fast.

Our Origin

Why We Started

The way Australians use the internet has completely changed, but traditional providers haven't kept up. We noticed homes struggling with dropouts during peak hours and businesses losing revenue due to slow upload speeds.

We founded Neo Broadband to fix this. We stripped away the confusing data tiers, hidden lock-in contracts, and frustrating automated support queues. Instead, we built a network focused on pure performance and straightforward, local service.

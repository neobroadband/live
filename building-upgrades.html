The Upgrade Journey

A seamless 5-stage end-to-end framework handled entirely by our specialists.

Step 1
Building Eligibility Check

We analyse your building’s position, installed infrastructure, and structural layout to determine the optimal deployment approach.

Step 2
Quote & Agreement

Providing a itemised quotation and project agreement for body corporate review.

Step 3
Survey & Network Design

Our technical team performs detailed on site mapping to develop a customised installation blueprint tailored to your building.

Step 4
Deployment & Installation

With pre start engagement completed with the body corporate, fibre pathways are deployed across the entire building, supported by fast, well coordinated appointments for every apartment.

Step 5
Connection Ready

A notification is sent to residents, prompting them to choose their preferred participating ISP and activate high speed fibre plans.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything strata managers and committees need to know.

Will my building qualify?

Most multi-dwelling properties can be assessed. We look at factors like building layout, existing conduit infrastructure, and geographical reach. The initial eligibility review is completely free.

How disruptive is the installation process?

Common area works are efficiently managed over a few weeks based on scale, while individual apartment setups are quick, taking under an hour per residence at scheduled times.

Who makes the decision — strata or individual owners?

The approval is coordinated through the body corporate or owners committee representing the community. Individual residents do not need to register separately for the backbone infrastructure rollout.

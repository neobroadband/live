General & Network Services

Learn about our network architecture and Australian support structure.

Neo Broadband is built from the ground up on high-capacity fiber routes designed to eliminate peak-hour congestion. Unlike traditional providers that rely on offshore support and lock-in agreements, we offer 100% Australian-based customer support, honest pricing without hidden fees, and month-to-month flexibility.
Our entire technical and customer care team is located right here in Australia. When you call or lodge an enquiry, you speak directly to qualified network experts who can diagnose and resolve issues immediately.
If your premises has an active nbn® connection box (NTD), activation is typically completed within 1 to 2 business hours. If an nbn® technician appointment is required for physical cabling or equipment installation, it usually takes 2 to 5 business days depending on your area.

Residential Broadband Plans

Details on download speeds, typical evening performance, and unlimited data.

No. All residential plans—including Basic (25/10), Regular (500/50), Ultra (1000/100), Pro, Ultra+, and Thunderbolt (2000/500)—come with unlimited data with zero speed shaping, data caps, or peak-time throttling.
Typical Evening Speeds are measured during the busiest usage period (7:00 PM – 11:00 PM). We purchase generous wholesale capacity (CVC) so our evening speeds remain exceptionally close to theoretical maximums (e.g., our 500Mbps Regular plan delivers a typical evening speed of 500/42 Mbps).
Our Pro (250/100 Mbps), Pro + (500/200 Mbps), and Ultra + (1000/400 Mbps) plans are tailored for content creators, remote engineers, video editors, and power users who need high upstream bandwidth for rapid cloud backups, video rendering, and heavy simultaneous uploading.

Business & Enterprise Ethernet

Static IPs, business SLAs, symmetric fiber, and priority routing.

Yes. Every Neo Business plan (from 50/20 Mbps up to 1000/400 Mbps) includes one complimentary static IPv4 address, essential for hosting on-premise servers, secure VPN tunnels, POS systems, and VoIP phone PBX systems. Additional IP subnets (/29) are available upon request.
nbn® Enterprise Ethernet is a premium, point-to-point dedicated fiber link with 1:1 symmetric upload/download speeds (from 100/100 Mbps up to 1000/1000 Mbps). It includes enterprise-grade Service Level Agreements (SLAs) with 4-hour to 12-hour restoration targets and optional 4G/5G failover for maximum business continuity.
Our Business nbn® plans feature 24/7 priority routing and support. For mission-critical operations requiring financial uptime guarantees, our Enterprise Ethernet solutions deliver a 99.95% availability SLA backed by rapid on-site technician dispatch.

Billing, Discounts & Contracts

Payment terms, annual discount savings, and no-contract policies.

No. All our standard residential and business nbn® subscriptions operate on transparent month-to-month terms. You are free to upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your service at any time without exit fees or penalty charges.
When opting for an annual upfront subscription, you receive a direct 5% discount across the full 12 months. For example, our 500Mbps Regular residential plan reduces from A$85/month to A$80.75/month (A$969 billed annually), saving you money each year.
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), automated Direct Debit (Bank Account), and BPAY. Invoices and payment receipts are available 24/7 via the Customer Portal.

Hardware, Modems & Wi-Fi Setup

BYO routers, Wi-Fi 6 hardware compatibility, and troubleshooting.
